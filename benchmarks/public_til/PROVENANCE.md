# Ground-Truth Data Provenance (`benchmarks/public_til`)

This document records the primary data deposits, supplementary label tables, experimental assays establishing ground truth, preprocessing transforms in `download.py`, chain-matching rules in `run.py`, and circularity disclosures for all five public tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) cohorts evaluated in `docs/benchmarks.md`.

## Cohort-by-Cohort Preprocessing & Label Mapping Rules

### 1. Meng et al. 2023 (`meng` — PDAC, `GSE254250`)
- **Raw files (`download.py`)**: Downloads 7 Cell Ranger `.h5` matrices (`249`, `262`, `282`, `309`, `418`, `432`, and `Pool`, where `Pool` holds multiplexed patients `TIPC301`, `TIPC413`, and `TIPC416`) plus `GSE254250_PDAC_9sample_CD8_RNA_metadata_matrix.csv.gz`.
- **Cell & clone construction (`run.py:meng_cells`)**: Subsets each `.h5` matrix to barcodes listed in the authors' CD8 metadata (`14,396` cells across 9 patients; columns `'Unnamed: 0'` `<patient>_<barcode>`, `'orig.ident'`, `'clonotype_id'`, `'Reactivity (n=149)'`) and normalizes expression with `sc.pp.normalize_total(..., target_sum=10_000)` + `sc.pp.log1p(...)`. Because GEO does not deposit raw VDJ contig CSVs for `GSE254250`, clones are keyed by the authors' per-cell `'clonotype_id'` identifier (`5,731` scored clones) and scored via the exact library functions used by `tcrsift prioritize` (`_score_within_samples`, `calibrate_signature_background`, `signature_pass_mask`, `select_round_robin`).
- **Ground-truth labels**: `TR` (`94` clones) vs `NTR` (`55` clones); `"-"` in metadata is mapped to `"untested"` (`5,582` clones).

### 2. Chiffelle et al. 2024 (`chiffelle` — Melanoma, `GSE222448`)
- **Raw files (`download.py`)**: Downloads dense `CD45-counts.tsv.gz` tables and `all_contig_annotations_Patient*_T0*.csv.gz` for all 13 baseline pre-therapy tumor biopsies (`T0`, `T01`, `T02`) of the 11 patients with functionally tested TCRs (`Patients 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13`), plus Supplementary Table S3 (`mmc3.xlsx`).
- **Format standardization (`download.py`)**:
  - `counts_table_to_matrix`: Converts each dense tab-delimited gene-by-cell count table into standard 10x Matrix Market (`matrix.mtx.gz`, `features.tsv.gz`, `barcodes.tsv.gz`), normalizing barcode suffixes to `-1` to match Cell Ranger VDJ barcodes.
  - `filtered_contigs`: Filters `all_contig_annotations` using Cell Ranger's exact filtered-contig predicate (`is_cell == "true"` and `high_confidence == "true"`).
- **Ground-truth labels (`run.py:chiffelle_labels`)**: Parses Table S3 (sheet `'clonotypes'`, `header=2`), forward-fills `'Patient'`, keeps rows with `'Tumor-reactivity'` in `{"yes", "no"}`, extracts `CDR3_beta` from column B (`'TCR β chain'`, formatted `V_CDR3_J`: `raw.iloc[:, 1].str.split("_").str[1].str[:-1]`, removing the trailing J-region residue beyond the canonical IMGT `F`/`W` anchor), and drops any `(donor, CDR3_beta)` listed with contradictory reactivity across different α partners (`one_label_per_beta`), yielding `123` `TR` and `124` `NTR` published β-clonotypes (`72` `TR` and `94` `NTR` scored after default `prioritize` cell filters).

### 3. Caushi et al. 2021 (`caushi` — NSCLC, `GSE176021`)
- **Raw files (`download.py`)**: Downloads Springer Supplementary Table 8 (`41586_2021_3752_MOESM8_ESM.xlsx`) and all paired GEX + VDJ Cell Ranger archives across tumor, brain/resected metastasis (`mettumor`), draining lymph node (`LN`), and adjacent normal lung (`normal`) for the 8 patients with antigen-validated TCRs (`MD01-004`, `MD01-005`, `MD043-003`, `MD043-008`, `MD043-011`, `NY016-007`, `NY016-014`, `NY016-025`). Handles GEO's double-gzipped `.vdj.tar.gz` archives transparently (`extract_flat`).
- **Ground-truth labels (`run.py:caushi_labels`)**: Reads sheet `'Supplementary Table 8'` (`iloc[3:, :3]`; header row 3 columns `'Patient'`, `'TCR clonotype (AA)'`, `'Type of antigen recognized (viral or MANA)'`), strips `*` characters from `CDR3_beta`, maps antigen categories containing `"MANA"` (case-insensitive) to `"MANA"` (`74` published β-chains) and remaining validated viral epitopes (CEF / Influenza / EBV / CMV) to `"viral"` (`82` published β-chains), deduplicating `(donor, CDR3_beta)`.
- **Chain collapsing (`collapse_by_beta`)**: Because MANAFEST provides β-chain specificity only, a labeled `CDR3_beta` paired with multiple α chains in scTCR-seq (dual-α T cells or α dropout) is counted once per `(donor, CDR3_beta)`, represented by its largest paired clone (tie-broken deterministically on `CDR3_alpha`) for continuous AUROC and marked passing/selected if any of its α-paired clones passed/was selected.

### 4. Eberhardt et al. 2021 (`eberhardt` — HPV+ HNSCC, `GSE180268`)
- **Raw files (`download.py`)**: Downloads all paired HLA-A\*01:01 HPV16 E2 (`QVDYYGLYY` and `KSAIVTLTY`) tetramer-sorted and bulk `PD1pos` CD8 samples for patients `HPV7`, `HPV34`, and `HPV51` (`6` bulk `PD1pos` samples: 3 primary tumor, 3 metastatic lymph node), converting `all_contig_annotations.csv.gz` via `filtered_contigs` (`is_cell == "true"` & `high_confidence == "true"`).
- **Ground-truth labels (`run.py:eberhardt_labels`)**: Identifies productive `TRB` CDR3s (`productive == "True"`) observed in `>=5` cells in a patient's HPV16 E2 tetramer-sorted libraries whose maximum relative frequency in tetramer sorts is greater than or equal to their maximum relative frequency in that patient's bulk `PD1pos` libraries (preventing abundant non-binding bulk clones carried over as sort contaminants from being mislabeled `HPV`). Evaluated against all unlabeled `>=2`-cell clones in bulk `PD1pos` libraries.

### 5. Pulliam et al. 2024 (`pulliam` — Merkel cell carcinoma, `GSE227708`)
- **Raw files (`download.py`)**: Downloads Table S4 (`mmc5.xlsx`), `GSE227708_meta_data.csv.gz`, and hashed 10x runs `Z1249` and `Z1308` (the two runs containing labeled clones in a tumor specimen whose hashtags match the deposited metadata sheet).
- **Hashtag demultiplexing (`run.py:pulliam_tumor_samples`)**: Assigns a cell barcode to its top `Hashtag*` oligo when `top_hashtag_umi >= 10` and `top_hashtag_umi >= 3 * max(second_hashtag_umi, 1)`, retains cells mapped to `tissue == "Tumor"` (`8,475` cells in `Z1249`, `350` in `Z1308`), strips the `GRCh38_` genome prefix from human gene symbols, and subsets `all_contig_annotations.csv.gz` to called, high-confidence contigs of those tumor barcodes.
- **Ground-truth labels (`run.py:pulliam_labels`)**: Maps Table S4 (sheet `'Table 4'`, `header=1`) `'subject'` tube identifiers to `Patient_id` via `GSE227708_meta_data.csv.gz`, labeling `'virus' == "MCPyV"` as `"MCPyV"` (`22` published in `Z1249`; `5` with `>=2` cells in tumor) and non-MCPyV viral specificities as `"other_virus"` (`13` in `Z1308`), dropping conflicting β labels via `one_label_per_beta` and collapsing across α partners per `(donor, CDR3_beta)` via `collapse_by_beta`.

## Reference Database Provenance

- **VDJdb (`vdjdb-2026-11-06`)**: `280,894` `TRB` rows (`200,180` paired α/β entries) cached via `tcrsift data download --db vdjdb` and used under `tcrsift prioritize`'s default `--database-match strict_ab` policy for known MART-1 (`MLANA`) exclusion across `chiffelle`, `caushi`, `eberhardt`, and `pulliam` (`--exclude-known-viral` ablation also evaluated on `caushi`).
