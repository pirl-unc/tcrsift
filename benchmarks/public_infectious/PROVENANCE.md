## Cohort-by-Cohort Ground-Truth Construction & Edge Cases

### 1. 10x Genomics Multiplexed 50-Dextramer Donors 1 & 2 (`tenx_dextramer`)
- **Cell-level binder calls**: Read directly from 10x Genomics' `vdj_v1_hs_aggregated_donor{1,2}_binarized_matrix.csv`. Only cells binding **exactly one** pMHC specificity (`nb == 1` across the 44 `*_binder` columns) are used as ground-truth cell labels, excluding promiscuous poly-reactive droplets.
- **Clonal ground-truth assignment**: Mapped onto paired `CDR3ab` clonotypes (`CDR3_alpha` + `CDR3_beta`) when **at least 50% of cells in the VDJ clone** share the modal single-binder call (`tenx_concordance >= 0.5`). For all expanded-clone (`>=2` cells) evaluations (`expanded_viral_vs_unlabeled_expanded`, `influenza_m1_vs_ebv_expanded`, `vdjdb_matching`, and `blood_cutoffs_and_shortlist`), positive clones additionally require at least 2 single-binder cells (`tenx_cells >= 2`), excluding 2-cell VDJ clones with only 1 binder cell.
- **Exclusion of known sticky reagent from viral truth**: `A0301_KLGGALQAK_IE-1_CMV` is a documented promiscuous pMHC reagent that binds isolated cells inside unrelated clones (including HLA-A\*03:01-negative donors and clones whose sibling cells concordantly bind Influenza or EBV dextramers). Ground-truth viral memory (`VIRAL_BONA_FIDE_RE = r"Influenza|EBV|pp65_CMV"`) therefore comprises Influenza A M1 (`A0201_GILGFVFTL_Flu-MP_Influenza`), EBV (`BMLF1`, `BZLF1`, `EBNA-3A`, `EBNA-3B`, `LMP2A`), and CMV pp65 (`A0201_NLVPMVATV_pp65_CMV`), while `A0301_KLGGALQAK_IE-1_CMV` is evaluated explicitly in `multimer_calling.csv` as a quality-control stress test.
- **Biological validation of naive negative controls**: `A0201_ELAGIGILTV_MART-1_Cancer` dextramer+ clones in healthy blood represent unprimed thymic naive precursors (`99.3%` singletons in Donor 1, `100%` in Donor 2). CITE-seq centered log-ratio (`CLR(x) = log1p(x) - mean(log1p(x))`) surface protein measurements confirm `CD45RA`-high / `CD45RO`-low naive phenotype (`mean CLR(CD45RO) - CLR(CD45RA) = -2.99` in Donor 1 and `-4.61` in Donor 2, ~4 CLR units below viral memory).

### 2. Minervina et al. 2022 (`minervina_2022` — Zenodo `6232103`)
- **Raw GEX + VDJ**: Extracted unchanged from `libs_aggregate_rev.zip` (`filtered_feature_bc_matrix.h5` and `filtered_contig_annotations.csv`).
- **Curated epitope & exposure stage labels**: Joined by cell `barcode` from `pogorely/COVID_vax_CD8/master/cd8_only_dextr_rev_clean.tsv` (`donor`, `epitope`, `spike`, `donor_category`, `sample_category`).
  - Five exposure categories (`donor_category`): `naive` (pre-exposure), `post_inf` (convalescent post-SARS-CoV-2 infection), `inf_after_1` (hybrid immunity after 1 mRNA dose), `inf_after_2` (acute recall shortly after 2nd mRNA vaccine dose), and `break` (breakthrough infection).
  - For the acute recall vs resting convalescent AUROC (`inf_after_2` vs `post_inf`), clones are restricted to `>=2` paired-TCR CD8 cells within the category, and **longitudinal clones observed in both `inf_after_2` and `post_inf` are excluded** (`shared_longitudinal_clones_excluded_from_auroc`) so positive and negative clone sets are strictly disjoint.

### 3. Fischer et al. 2021 (`fischer_2021` — GEO `GSE171037`)
- **Sample pairing**:
  - Donor `GT_6`: `GSM5216975` (`Sample_20063a009_01`, unstimulated PBMC control) paired with `GSM5216976` (`Sample_20063a010_01`, 16 h SARS-CoV-2 Spike peptide pool).
  - Donor `GT_2`: `GSM5216977` (`Sample_20063a011_01`, unstimulated PBMC control) paired with `GSM5216978` (`Sample_20063a012_01`, 16 h SARS-CoV-2 Spike peptide pool).
- **5'-truncated Cell Ranger 3 VDJ contigs**: `98.7%`–`99.7%` of called TCR contigs in `GSE171037` have `full_length == False` and `productive == "None"` because 5' sequencing terminated downstream of the V-leader start codon despite complete `v_gene` and `j_gene` alignments and stop-free canonical `C...[FW]` CDR3 sequences (`productive == "False"` marks genuine out-of-frame/stop contigs). `tcrsift.loader.load_cellranger_vdj` rescues validated `productive == "None"` contigs while marking `_is_full_productive = False` so full-length contigs take precedence whenever present.
- **CD3 T-cell gating prior to normalization**: Unsorted PBMCs are gated to T cells (`phenotype_cells` + `apply_cd3_gate(..., min_cd3_reads=3)`) *before* `normalize_total` and `score_by_name` so monocytes, NK cells, and B cells do not distort background gene-expression bins or non-T `TNF`/`CCL4` counts.

### 4. Reference Database Provenance (VDJdb)
- Downloaded via `tcrsift data download --db vdjdb` from `antigenomics/vdjdb-db` latest GitHub release (`vdjdb-2026-11-06`, relational `chains.tsv.gz` + `records.tsv.gz`: `280,894` TRB entries, `200,180` paired α/β entries).
- Exact version and entry counts used in each run are stamped into `benchmarks/public_infectious/results/summary.json` (`vdjdb_release`, `vdjdb_entries`, `vdjdb_paired_ab_entries`).
