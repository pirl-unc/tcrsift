# Ground-Truth Data Provenance (`benchmarks/public_blood_cd4`)

This document records the primary open-access data deposits, sample-to-patient mappings, experimental assays establishing biological ground truth, preprocessing and clonal aggregation rules in `run.py`, reference database versions, and methodological disclosures for all four human cohorts (`--context blood-tumor`, longitudinal post-ACT `T30`, and `--tcell-type cd4`) evaluated in `docs/benchmarks-blood-cd4.md`.

## Cohort-by-Cohort Preprocessing & Edge Cases

### 1. Pulliam et al. 2024 Paired Blood + Tumor MCC (`pulliam_blood` — `GSE227708`)
- **Per-tube hashtag demultiplexing**: Each run (`Z1210`, `Z1249`, `Z1308`, `Z1493`, `Z1493B`) contains `Hashtag1`–`Hashtag8` rows in `features.tsv.gz` mapped via `GSE227708_meta_data.csv.gz` to individual blood draws or tumor biopsies (`Tube`, `Patient_id`, `tissue`, `Note`). Following `benchmarks/public_til/run.py`, a cell is assigned to its top hashtag when `top_count >= 10` and `top_count >= 3 * max(runner_up, 1)`. Any demultiplexed library with `>=20` cells is written as a standard 10x sample (`10` Blood samples and `2` Tumor samples with `>=20` cells out of `15` Blood and `4` Tumor hashtag rows in `meta_data.csv.gz` across `Z1210`, `Z1249`, `Z1308`, and `Z1493`; exact per-hashtag cell counts are recorded in `results/summary.json`).
- **Table S4 specificity join**: `mmc5.xlsx` (`Table 4`) lists verified `MCPyV`, `CMV`, `EBV`, and `Flu` CD8+ TCRs with `subject` tube identifiers (`MCCLy-1200`, `MCCLy-1033`, `MCCLy-1192`, `MCCLy-1000`, `TD6222`, etc.) mapped via normalized `Tube` names to `Patient_id` (`Z1210`, `Z1249`, `Z1308`, `Z1493`). Any `CDR3_beta` with contradictory `MCPyV` vs `control_virus` calls within a donor is excluded.

### 2. Chiffelle / Barras et al. 2024 Pre-ACT `T0` vs Post-ACT `T30` Melanoma (`chiffelle_act` — `GSE222448`)
- **Matched longitudinal cohort**: Six patients in `GSE222448` (`Patients 1, 5, 7, 8, 10, 13`) have both baseline pre-ACT tumor biopsies (`T0`, `T01`, `T02`; 8 samples total) and Day-30 post-ACT tumor biopsies (`T30`; 6 samples total) alongside autologous tumor co-culture `TR` vs `NTR` labels in Table S3 (`mmc3.xlsx`). Both timepoints are run identically with `tcrsift prioritize --context solid-tumor` so `T0` and `T30` AUROCs and shortlist precision are compared on the exact same 6 patients.

### 3. Veatch et al. 2022 Melanoma Neoantigen CD4+ TILs (`veatch_cd4` — `GSE198265`)
- **Separation of BD Rhapsody mRNA and AbSeq columns**: `*_DBEC_MolsPerCell.csv.gz` concatenates `397` targeted mRNA genes with `37` AbSeq oligo-conjugated antibody columns (`*|pAbO`). All `|pAbO` columns are stripped prior to `normalize_total(target_sum=10_000)` and `log1p` so protein tag counts never distort mRNA library-size normalization or gene-expression module scores.
- **Sample-tag hygiene & dominant Vβ clonotypes**: `Multiplet` and `Undetermined` sample tags are removed (`X198` is restricted to the resected tumor `8_26_main_harvest`, excluding `8_4_lymph_node`; `X422` retains `tumorA` and `tumorB` as separate tumor strata). Because the deposited BD Rhapsody VDJ table provides `TCR_Beta_Delta_CDR3_Translation_Dominant` (`TCRVb? == True`, without α-chain sequences or conserved N/C anchors), TCRsift's exact `score_by_name(..., on_missing="ignore")`, `calibrate_signature_background`, `signature_pass_mask`, and `select_round_robin` functions are called directly on experimental CD4+ T cells (`Cell_Type_Experimental` in `{"T_CD4_memory", "T_CD4_naive"}`).

### 4. Oh et al. 2020 Whole-Transcriptome Bladder CD4+ TILs (`oh_cd4` — `GSE149652`)
- **Whole-transcriptome Cell Ranger `hg19` symbol harmonization**: `GSE149652` was aligned against 10x Cell Ranger `hg19` (GENCODE v19), where *ADGRG1* and *LINC01943* in `NeoTCR4` appear under their former official symbols *GPR56* and *RP11-291B21.2*. `tcrsift.genes.GENE_SYNONYMS` resolves both automatically, providing **40/40 (`100%`)** `NeoTCR4` gene coverage under strict `on_missing="error"`.
- **FACS `pre_sorted=CD4` coreceptor-dropout rescue**: All 13 libraries in `GSE149652` were physically FACS-sorted for `CD3+ CD4+ CD8-` T cells prior to 10x capture, yet `81.4%`–`96.5%` of `CD3 >= 3` cells across individual libraries (`88.5%` overall) have `0` `CD4` mRNA UMIs due to low *CD4* transcript copy number in human droplet scRNA-seq. Passing `pre_sorted=CD4` on the sample sheet retains `CD3 >= 3, CD4 == 0, CD8 == 0` cells as `"Likely CD4+"` while still rejecting any `CD8 > 0` sorting impurities (`0.0%`–`3.8%` across the 13 libraries). Both `pre_sorted=CD4` and `pre_sorted=None` (`CD4 mRNA > 0` only) are evaluated directly in `shortlist_oh_cd4.csv`.
